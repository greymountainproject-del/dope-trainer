# Dope Trainer

A precision rifle match simulator for your phone. It calls stages (distance, wind speed and clock direction, movers, multiple positions), times them, and grades your elevation and wind calls against your own dope card, using realistic plate sizes.

**Open it:** https://greymountainproject-del.github.io/dope-trainer/

- Enter your dope from a screenshot of your ballistic app or by hand (saved on your phone only)
- Match mode: 1–10 timed stages with a scorecard
- Impact plot that tracks where your shots land and your wind and elevation tendencies
- Optional mic shot counter (works on the https link in Safari or Chrome)

⚠ Training aid only. Always follow safe gun handling and range rules. When dry firing, verify the rifle is unloaded and keep live ammo out of the room.
