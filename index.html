How the mic works ▾
  1. It only works from a web link (https). Put this file on Netlify Drop or GitHub Pages and open the link in Safari or Chrome. It won't work opened from the Files app or inside Claude.
  2. Tap Test mic and allow microphone access. The blue bar shows how loud it is.
  3. Clap or dry fire next to the phone, then drag the red line above the background noise and below your shot. Watch for "💥 Shot detected."
  4. At the range, set the phone beside you or behind the rifle, within about 3 ft. Keep it out of the brake blast. A suppressed rifle is quieter, so lower the line.
  5. On a busy firing line, raise the line to 0.6–0.8 so only your own shot counts.
  6. The mic is optional. During a live stage it just counts your shots on screen. You never have to tap anything mid-stage. Shoot it all, then tap Stage complete.
  7. Voice commands use the same mic. Say "ready" to start a stage. During the stage, say "next", "back", "target 3" or "repeat" to change targets and hear the call. If they don't respond, use the buttons. iPhone only allows voice commands in Safari, and you may need to allow speech recognition once.
